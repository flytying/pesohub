## BPI Time Deposit Interest Rates (2026)

Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI) offers two main peso time deposit products:
the **BPI Standard Time Deposit** and the **BPI Peso Auto-Renew Time Deposit**.
Each has different rate structures, tenors, and minimum placement amounts.

All rates shown are **gross rates per annum (p.a.) before the 20% final withholding tax (FWT)**
that the Philippine government applies to interest income on bank deposits.

---

### BPI Standard Time Deposit Rates

The BPI Standard Time Deposit is straightforward: you lock in a fixed amount for a chosen tenor
and earn interest at a flat rate.

| Tenor | Gross Interest Rate (p.a.) | Minimum Placement |
|-------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| 6 months (182 days) | 2.75% | ₱50,000 |
| 12 months (365 days) | 3.25% | ₱50,000 |

> **Note:** These are gross rates before the 20% final withholding tax. Your **net rate** after
> tax is approximately **2.20% p.a. (6-month)** and **2.60% p.a. (12-month)**.

### Worked Example: ₱100,000 in a BPI Standard Time Deposit (12 months)

Let's say you place **₱100,000** in a BPI Standard Time Deposit for **12 months** at 3.25% p.a.

**Step 1 — Compute gross interest:**

```
Gross Interest = Principal × Rate × (Days / 365)
Gross Interest = ₱100,000 × 3.25% × (365 / 365)
Gross Interest = ₱100,000 × 0.0325
Gross Interest = ₱3,250.00
```

**Step 2 — Deduct 20% final withholding tax:**

```
Tax = ₱3,250.00 × 20% = ₱650.00
Net Interest = ₱3,250.00 − ₱650.00 = ₱2,600.00
```

**Step 3 — Total amount at maturity:**

```
Maturity Value = ₱100,000 + ₱2,600.00 = ₱102,600.00
```

After one year, your ₱100,000 placement grows to **₱102,600.00 (net of withholding tax)**.

### Is BPI Time Deposit PDIC-Insured?

Yes. BPI is a member of the **Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation (PDIC)**. Your BPI time
deposit is insured by PDIC for up to **₱500,000 per depositor** across all your deposit accounts
at BPI. Amounts above ₱500,000 are not covered by PDIC insurance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is BPI's current time deposit interest rate?

As of 2026, BPI's Standard Time Deposit offers **2.75% p.a. (gross) for a 6-month term**
and **3.25% p.a. (gross) for a 12-month term**, with a minimum placement of ₱50,000.
BPI's Auto-Renew Time Deposit offers lower rates (0.25%–0.75% p.a. gross) that vary by
balance tier and tenor. All interest is subject to the 20% final withholding tax.
Rates are subject to change — verify the latest rates directly with BPI.

---

### What is the minimum amount for a BPI time deposit?

The minimum placement for both the BPI Standard Time Deposit and the BPI Peso Auto-Renew
Time Deposit is **₱50,000**. Rates improve at higher balance tiers (₱500,000; ₱1,000,000;
₱5,000,000 and above).

---

### How is interest on a BPI time deposit taxed in the Philippines?

Interest earned on a BPI time deposit is subject to **20% final withholding tax (FWT)**
under Philippine tax law. This tax is automatically deducted by the bank before interest
is credited to you. You do not need to include this income in your annual BIR income tax
return because it has already been withheld at source.

---

### How do I open a BPI time deposit?

You can open a BPI time deposit through the following channels:

1. **BPI Online or BPI Mobile App** — Existing BPI account holders can open a time deposit
   directly through the app or online banking portal. Look for the "Time Deposit" or
   "Invest" section.
2. **BPI Branch** — Visit any BPI branch with a valid government-issued ID and your initial
   placement amount. A BPI representative will assist you in choosing a tenor and product.
3. **BPI Banker / Relationship Manager** — For placements of ₱500,000 and above, you may
   be assigned a relationship manager who can facilitate placement.

> Requirements and procedures may change. Check the [official BPI website](https://www.bpi.com.ph)
> or call BPI's hotline for the most current process.

---

### What is the difference between BPI's Standard Time Deposit and Auto-Renew Time Deposit?

The **Standard Time Deposit** offers higher flat rates (2.75%–3.25% p.a.) on two specific
tenors (6 and 12 months). The **Peso Auto-Renew Time Deposit** offers shorter, more flexible
tenors (35 to 365 days) but at significantly lower rates (0.25%–0.75% p.a. gross), tiered
by balance. If your goal is to maximize yield on a medium-term placement, the Standard Time
Deposit generally offers better rates.

---

### Are BPI time deposit rates negotiable?

For retail placements (under ₱5,000,000), BPI typically applies its posted rates.
For very large placements, you may be able to negotiate a special rate with your
relationship manager or branch. Rates are set by BPI and may change at any time
without prior notice.

---

### How does a BPI time deposit compare to a regular savings account?

A BPI time deposit typically offers **higher interest rates** than a regular BPI savings
account, in exchange for locking your money for a fixed period. The trade-off is liquidity:
withdrawing a time deposit before maturity may result in **reduced interest or penalties**.
If you want to compare where your savings can earn more, see our guide to the
[best savings interest rates in the Philippines](/rates/savings-rates/best-savings-interest-rates-philippines).
