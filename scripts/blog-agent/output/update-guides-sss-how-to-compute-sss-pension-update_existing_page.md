## How Is Your SSS Pension Computed?

The SSS uses **three formulas** to calculate your monthly retirement pension. Your pension will be whichever of the three produces the **highest amount**.

Before applying any formula, you need to know two numbers:

- **AMSC (Average Monthly Salary Credit):** The average of your salary credits over your entire SSS membership, capped at **₱20,000** for the regular pension computation. (Salary credits above ₱20,000 and up to ₱35,000 go toward the WISP provident fund benefit, not the regular monthly pension.)
- **CYS (Credited Years of Service):** The total number of years you have paid SSS contributions.

---

### The Three SSS Pension Formulas

| Formula | Computation | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Formula 1** | ₱300 + (20% × AMSC) + [2% × AMSC × (CYS − 10)] | Members with longer contribution histories |
| **Formula 2** | 40% × AMSC | Members with shorter contribution histories |
| **Formula 3** | Minimum pension | Members with very low salary credits |

Your monthly pension = **the highest result among the three formulas**.

---

### Formula 1 — Explained

**₱300 + (20% × AMSC) + [2% × AMSC × (CYS − 10)]**

This formula rewards members who have contributed for many years. The more Credited Years of Service you have beyond 10 years, the higher this formula's result.

- The base amount is a fixed **₱300**.
- Add **20%** of your AMSC as a base pension component.
- Add **2% of your AMSC** for each year of service beyond the first 10 years.

Formula 1 is usually the highest result for members who have paid SSS contributions for 20 years or more.

---

### Formula 2 — Explained

**40% × AMSC**

This is a simpler calculation. It gives you 40% of your Average Monthly Salary Credit as your monthly pension. This formula tends to produce the highest result for members who have a higher AMSC but fewer years of service.

---

### Formula 3 — Minimum Pension

The SSS guarantees a **minimum monthly pension** regardless of your AMSC or CYS, as long as you meet the minimum contribution requirements:

| Credited Years of Service | Minimum Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| 10 years to under 20 years | **₱1,200** |
| 20 years or more | **₱2,400** |

An **across-the-board increase of ₱1,000** is added to the minimum pension amount on top of the figures above, based on the most recent SSS pension adjustment.

> **Verify current rates:** The minimum pension figures and any across-the-board increases are set by SSS and may be updated by law or SSS resolution. Confirm the current minimum pension with the official SSS website or your nearest SSS branch before making retirement plans.
```

## SSS Pension Computation Example (Philippines)

Here is a worked example using a typical Filipino worker's profile.

---

### Sample Member Profile

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Average Monthly Salary Credit (AMSC) | ₱15,000 |
| Credited Years of Service (CYS) | 25 years |

---

### Step 1 — Apply Formula 1

**₱300 + (20% × ₱15,000) + [2% × ₱15,000 × (25 − 10)]**

- 20% × ₱15,000 = **₱3,000**
- 2% × ₱15,000 = ₱300 per additional year
- CYS beyond 10 years = 25 − 10 = **15 years**
- 15 × ₱300 = **₱4,500**
- Formula 1 result = ₱300 + ₱3,000 + ₱4,500 = **₱7,800**

---

### Step 2 — Apply Formula 2

**40% × ₱15,000 = ₱6,000**

---

### Step 3 — Check Minimum Pension

This member has 25 Credited Years of Service (20 or more), so the minimum pension is **₱2,400**.

---

### Step 4 — Choose the Highest Result

| Formula | Result |
|---|---|
| Formula 1 | ₱7,800 |
| Formula 2 | ₱6,000 |
| Minimum Pension | ₱2,400 |

✅ **Estimated monthly SSS pension: ₱7,800** (Formula 1 gives the highest result)

---

> **Note:** This example is for illustration only. Your actual pension depends on your official SSS contribution records and the rules in effect at the time you claim your retirement benefit. Use the [SSS Pension Calculator](#) above to run your own numbers, or visit the SSS to get an official computation.

## What Is the Minimum SSS Pension in the Philippines?

The SSS sets a **guaranteed minimum monthly pension** to protect retirees with lower salary credits or fewer years of contribution.

| Credited Years of Service | Minimum Monthly Pension |
|---|---|
| At least 10 years but less than 20 years | **₱1,200 per month** |
| 20 years or more | **₱2,400 per month** |

Even if your computed pension from Formula 1 or Formula 2 falls below these amounts, you will still receive the applicable minimum pension — as long as you meet the minimum CYS requirement.

Members who have contributed for **less than 10 years** are not eligible for a monthly pension. Instead, they receive a **lump-sum benefit** equal to their total contributions plus interest.

> Confirm the current minimum pension rates with the [SSS official website](https://www.sss.gov.ph) or your nearest branch, as these amounts may be adjusted by law or SSS resolution.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions About SSS Pension in the Philippines

---

### How is the SSS pension computed in the Philippines?

Your SSS monthly pension is computed using three formulas: Formula 1 (₱300 + 20% of AMSC + 2% of AMSC for each year of service beyond 10), Formula 2 (40% of AMSC), and the minimum pension (₱1,200 for 10–19 CYS or ₱2,400 for 20+ CYS). The SSS pays you whichever formula gives the highest result.

---

### What is the SSS pension formula in the Philippines?

The three SSS pension formulas are:
- **Formula 1:** ₱300 + (20% × AMSC) + [2% × AMSC × (CYS − 10)]
- **Formula 2:** 40% × AMSC
- **Formula 3 (Minimum Pension):** ₱1,200 (10–19 CYS) or ₱2,400 (20+ CYS)

Your pension is the highest of the three.

---

### How much SSS pension will I get after 10 years of contributions?

If you have exactly 10 Credited Years of Service, SSS will compute your pension using all three formulas and pay the highest result. With only 10 CYS, the minimum pension of ₱1,200 per month often applies unless your AMSC is high enough for Formula 1 or Formula 2 to exceed it. Use the SSS Pension Calculator at the top of this page to estimate your specific pension based on your AMSC.

---

### What is the minimum SSS pension in the Philippines?

The minimum monthly SSS pension is:
- **₱1,200** per month for members with 10 to under 20 Credited Years of Service
- **₱2,400** per month for members with 20 or more Credited Years of Service

Members with fewer than 10 CYS do not receive a monthly pension — they receive a lump-sum benefit instead.

---

### What is AMSC and how is it calculated?

AMSC stands for **Average Monthly Salary Credit**. It is the average of all your monthly salary credits over your SSS membership period. Your salary credit is based on your declared monthly earnings, and it is capped at **₱20,000** for the regular pension computation. Higher salary credits up to ₱35,000 are allocated to the WISP (Workers' Investment and Savings Program) provident fund, not the regular monthly pension. Use the [SSS Contribution Calculator](/calculators/sss/sss-contribution-calculator-philippines) to understand how your contributions relate to your salary credit.

---

### How do I use the SSS Pension Calculator?

Enter your Average Monthly Salary Credit (AMSC) and your total Credited Years of Service (CYS) into the calculator at the top of this page. The calculator will apply all three SSS pension formulas and show you the estimated monthly pension you may receive at retirement. The result is an estimate — your official pension is determined by the SSS based on your actual contribution records.

---

### Is the SSS pension taxable in the Philippines?

SSS retirement pensions are generally exempt from income tax in the Philippines under the National Internal Revenue Code. However, if you have questions about your specific tax situation, consult a licensed tax professional or verify with the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR).

---

### What happens to salary credits above ₱20,000?

For the regular SSS monthly pension, salary credits are capped at **₱20,000**. If your monthly salary credit is higher than ₱20,000 (up to ₱35,000), the amount above ₱20,000 is allocated to the **WISP (Workers' Investment and Savings Program)** provident fund. WISP benefits are paid separately from your regular monthly pension.
